  `linz-chc-2025-0075` (it overwrites the finished 60-window selection); check `crops.json gsd_m / native_window_px` before
  any judge.

  jobs for load.
- `jobgate`'s process pattern must not match TypeScript language servers or the read-only counter (fixed twice tonight).
- `wc -l` on macOS left-pads its count; pipe through `tr -d ' '` before `grep -qx 0`.
- `scene_confidence.wilson` rejects numpy integers: cast with `int()`.
- Matched-view summaries carry `arm_thresholds` at the top level (`meta` is null); pairing arms come from each run's
  `context['pairs']`.
- Hot-reloading `src/` while the reviewer saves: new files are safe; existing files only behind the five-minute no-save
  check, written whole, curl 200 after.
- LINZ 5 cm captures cover small extents only (2021 CBD, 2025 inner suburbs); the 2025 5 cm and 7.5 cm products share photons.
